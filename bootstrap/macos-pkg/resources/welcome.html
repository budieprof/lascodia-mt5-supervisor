Connect this Mac to Lascodia

This installs the Lascodia supervisor, a small helper that keeps your MetaTrader 5 terminals running and connected to your organisation.

  • It downloads the supervisor and checks that it is the genuine file.
  • It installs it for you (the person signed in now) and starts it every time you sign in.
  • It finds the MetaTrader 5 terminals on this Mac and connects them. Nothing else on this Mac is changed, and your terminals are not stopped.

It takes about a minute. Keep the console page open: it shows this Mac connecting.

Already installed? Running this again updates it and keeps its settings.
